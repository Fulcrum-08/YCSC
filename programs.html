Mass Save Programs

Through the Mass Save® Community First Partnership, Northampton residents can access no-cost and reduced-cost energy upgrades that improve comfort, lower utility bills, and reduce emissions.

Available Programs For Everyone

No-Cost Home Energy Assessment

A free evaluation of your home's energy use and savings opportunities.

This is the first step in the Mass Save program. A specialist evaluates your home and identifies ways to improve efficiency.

  • 100% free for most households
  • Includes instant energy-saving upgrades
  • Unlocks access to rebates and incentives

No-Cost Air Sealing

Seals leaks to prevent heat loss and drafts.

Air sealing is often provided at no cost and reduces energy waste immediately by closing gaps in your home.

  • Improves comfort
  • Reduces heating and cooling costs
  • Done during or after assessment

Heat Pump Incentives

Rebates for efficient electric heating and cooling systems.

Heat pumps are highly efficient systems that replace fossil fuel heating. Mass Save offers strong rebates depending on eligibility.

  • Heating and cooling in one system
  • Lower long-term costs
  • Large rebates available

Insulation Upgrades

Keeps homes warm in winter and cool in summer.

Insulation works with air sealing to reduce energy loss and improve efficiency year-round.

  • Attic, wall, and basement insulation
  • Lower utility bills
  • Long-term savings

0% HEAT Loan

Interest-free financing for energy improvements.

The HEAT Loan helps cover upfront costs for upgrades like heat pumps and insulation.

  • 0% interest financing
  • Makes upgrades more affordable
  • Available after assessment
