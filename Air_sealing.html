Air Sealing in Northampton, MA

Energy Savings, ROI, and Local Programs

🏠 What is Air Sealing?

Air sealing is the process of reducing uncontrolled air leakage through a building envelope. Common leakage pathways include attic penetrations, rim joists, window and door frames, and utility openings.

From a building science perspective, air leakage increases heat transfer through convection and infiltration. In cold climates like Massachusetts, this leads to significant winter heat loss and higher energy demand.

Air sealing is typically the first step in weatherization because it improves the performance of insulation and heating systems by stabilizing indoor air conditions.

📉 Energy Savings from Air Sealing

Studies from the U.S. Department of Energy and state programs show:

  • Air sealing reduces heating energy use by 10–15%
  • Greater savings occur when combined with insulation
